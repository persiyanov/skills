# Guard the Context Window

Keep the evidence and decisions you need in reach. Large outputs and repeated reads crowd out useful context.

- Search, filter, and read relevant sections before loading whole files or payloads.
- Use focused delegation when it helps, accounting for the effort of coordination.
- Keep access to original sources. Summaries save space but can lose details that matter.
- Put instructions needed on every invocation in the skill itself; keep occasional detail in references.
- Before a handoff or loss of context, record the objective, constraints, decisions, evidence locations, and remaining work.

Ask: What do I need in reach to finish, and what is crowding it out?
