# Encode Lessons in Structure

Change what allows a recurring mistake. A type, shared operation, or automated check can remove the need to remember a rule on every use.

First work out what generalizes. One example or preference is not automatically a universal rule. Put the fix where the relevant knowledge belongs, and choose a mechanism that prevents the actual mistake without burdening unrelated work.

Remove instructions made redundant by the fix, but keep explanations people still need. When the decision requires judgment, write a short principle rather than accumulating exceptions and process steps.

Acknowledging a lesson does not make it persistent. Make the authorized change or propose it clearly.

Ask: What prevents the same mistake next time?
